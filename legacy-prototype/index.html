    Prototype scope

    • Meal logging is live: camera or upload with an on-device food model, browser voice input, barcode scan, and nutrient lookup from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts.
    • Lab photo reading and Ask Remitra are still simulated with fixed demo responses.
    • Insights and flare outlook show synthetic results; target outcomes to be validated during discovery.
    • Meal photos stay in the browser. Food names and barcodes are sent to USDA and Open Food Facts for lookup; voice goes to the browser's speech service. Don't enter real health data.
    • Working name only; not affiliated with or endorsed by any federal agency.
